    Questions, answered.

    How do I start a chat?

    Tap Sender to show your QR code and ask the other person to tap Receiver and scan it. Or tap New chat and enter their phone number.

    Do I need a phone number?

    Only for chats by number. Add your own number first so people can reply to you. QR chats do not need one.

    What can I send?

    Text, voice messages, stickers, photos, files, links and your location.

    What if I lose my connection?

    Redapp keeps your messages and sends them when you are back online.

    Which devices does it run on?

    Redapp is available as an Android APK for now.

    The install is blocked. What now?

    Open your Android settings and allow installs from the app you used to download the file, then open the APK again.
